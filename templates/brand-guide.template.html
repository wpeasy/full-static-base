Motion

{{MOTION_PERSONALITY}} Movement is opt-in: everything that moves sits inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference; with reduced motion, content simply appears.

Accessibility

The site is built to WCAG 2.2 AA. These commitments apply to every page and every future change.

  • Semantic HTML first: one h1 per page, headings in order, landmarks, skip link.
  • Text on any brand colour uses its -a11y partner; meaning is never carried by colour alone.
  • Everything works by keyboard with a visible focus ring (--focus-ring); targets are at least 24×24 px, ideally 44.
  • Every control has a label and an accessible name; errors and hints are linked with aria-describedby.
  • Content reflows at 320px and 200% zoom; light, dark and forced-colours modes are supported.
  • Reduced motion: no movement, no autoplay.
