01 · Brand idea

Tough. Local. Beautiful up close.

The banksia is a hardy Gold Coast native whose seed pod opens only after fire. It is resilient, local and far more beautiful when you look closely. That is how Banksia Build Co. wants its work to feel: built to cope with salt, sun and storms, and finished with real craft.

The mark is a square-edged B: a teal spine like a carpenter's square edge, two warm bowls like timber ends. Confident, simple, and it works at favicon size.

04 · Logo

A B that stands up.

Clear space: the width of the spine on all sides. Minimum size: mark 20 px, horizontal lockup 120 px wide. Use on-dark on Ink/Slate, on-light on Sand, white/black on photography and brand-colour fills. Never recolour, outline, stretch or add effects.

Motion

Calm and confident, like a tide. Fade-and-rise entrances, slow image reveals, a gentle parallax on the hero sun. Everything that moves sits inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference; with reduce, content simply appears.

Iconography & shape

Chunky 2 px line icons with square caps, echoing a carpenter's pencil. Corners: 10–14 px radius on cards and buttons, sharp 90° on the set-square motif. Dividers use a thin Ember rule or the blueprint grid.
